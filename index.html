Objective 1: Extent of Coverage & Access
Policy, Plans, Inst. Mechanism & Finance
  • CMP: Prepared (not integrated); Not finalized/notified; Not updated.
  • TOD: Included in master plan but not notified.
  • Funds: Urban Transport Fund created in some, not created in others; Low utilization of funds.
Accessibility & Infrastructure
  • Assessment done in silos; Limited dynamic route rationalization.
  • Shortage of city buses; Sub-optimal fleet utilization; Absence of PT in peripheral areas.
  • No organised IPT and Feeder Buses; High Dependence on IPT.
  • Lack of Multimodal Integration; CMC not operationalised.
Objective 2: Quality of Urban Transport
Affordability & Comfort
  • Fare disparity; Weak regulation of IPT fare.
  • Absence of directional signages; No real time Passenger Information System.
  • Lack of general passenger amenities.
  • GRM: Available, but severe lack of awareness.
Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) & ICCC
  • Real-time passenger demand tracking app available, but ICCC not fully operationalised.
  • Punctuality data is neither monitored nor disclosed.
  • ITS integration not done; Very limited ITS enabled buses.
Objective 3: Traffic & Congestion
Traffic Management
  • Personnel deployment – insufficient, junction-based.
  • Low rate of traffic signal synchronisation; Traffic signals switched off at peak hours.
  • No dedicated lanes, inadequate road marking.
Parking Policies & Enforcement
  • No provisions for parking in building byelaws.
  • Unrealised parking fees and user charges.
  • Limited/No multi-level parking.
  • Congestion charging not fully implemented.
Objective 4: Sustainable & NMT
Sustainable Urban Transport
  • Non preparation of action plans; Non availing Central/State Assistance.
  • Slow progress of alternate fuel/ EV adoption; Lack of EV charging infra.
  • Unavailable sustainable last mile connectivity.
  • Inadequate enforcement of emission control measures; Non availability of IT enabled system.
Non-Motorised Transport (NMT)
  • Insufficient dedicated walking/cycling space; Non achievement of network targets.
  • Encroached/ Discontinuous footpaths & cycling tracks.
  • Lack of inclusive and universal accessible NMT infrastructure.
Objective 5: Safe & Secure Mobility
Safety Infrastructure
  • Deviations from standard pedestrian design standards.
  • Signages and markings not consistently provided.
  • Insufficient lighting on pedestrian paths.
  • CCTVs not integrated with ICCC.
Processes & Institutional Mechanism
  • Gaps in regulatory and enforcement mechanism.
  • Irregular meetings and limited systematic review by District Road Safety Committee.
  • Lack of periodic inspections of vehicles & Non-regular safety audits.
  • Unstructured awareness campaigns.
